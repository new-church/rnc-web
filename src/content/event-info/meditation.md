Kathryn Baker has been guiding members of our community in meditation for many years. Recently there has been an upsurge in activity and Kathryn has returned to a regular Monday morning meditation held in the quiet of the church sanctuary. In today's ever-changing, fast-paced and sometimes overwhelming life, meditation offers a gentle respite, an opportunity to re-centre, quiet the mind and become more aware of the comforting presence of the Divine. Even a short time of stillness can soften anxiety, renew focus, and deepen our capacity to love and serve.

If interested, feel free to join the group any Monday at 10:00am. If you'd like to speak with Kathryn before coming along, feel free to contact her on [0410 166 482](tel:+61410166482).

The group generally meets during the school term, and Kathryn is sometimes away, so the calendar shows which Mondays are on and which are cancelled.

## Before you come

Please check with Kathryn first that the group is meeting as usual, as details may change.
