Our friends at [Hurstville New Church](https://hurstvillenewchurch.com/) are holding a carol service on the evening of Sunday 20 December, and members and friends of the Roseville church are warmly welcome to go along. It is a lovely way to sing in the Christmas season in good company.

The service is at Hurstville New Church, 22-24 Dudley Street, Penshurst NSW 2222 (phone 02 9580-1589). It is a fair way from Roseville, so if you would like to go and would be glad of company or a lift, please get in touch with the church office at [admin@rosevillenewchurch.com.au](mailto:admin@rosevillenewchurch.com.au). We will do our best to put people in touch so that you can travel together.

For more about the Hurstville church, see their website at [hurstvillenewchurch.com](https://hurstvillenewchurch.com/).
