Join us for our Christmas service, followed by a lunch that the church provides. You are warmly invited, and friends and family are very welcome too.

If you are coming, please let us know so that we can plan the lunch. You can email the church office at [admin@rosevillenewchurch.com.au](mailto:admin@rosevillenewchurch.com.au) and it will reach Liz Kemmis.

That evening, our friends at Hurstville New Church are also holding a [carol service](/events/hurstville-carol-service) at 7pm. You are welcome to go along, and we can help put people in touch to share a lift.

If you have not been to one of our services before, the [Sunday worship page](/events/sunday-worship) explains what to expect.
