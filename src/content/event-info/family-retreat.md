**22 to 26 January 2027.** The Tops Conference Centre, 51 Bendena Gardens, Stanwell Tops, NSW.

You're warmly invited to join us for the 2027 New Church in Australia Family Retreat.

Whether you're coming for the first time or returning to reconnect with old friends, the retreat offers a welcoming space to step away from the busyness of everyday life, deepen your spiritual life, and enjoy the company of people of all ages.

Over five days you'll find a relaxed blend of inspiring talks and discussions, worship, creative and recreational activities, shared meals, and plenty of opportunities for conversation, laughter, and quiet reflection. Families, couples, singles, and people who are simply interested in exploring spirituality are all welcome.

You are welcome to stay for the entire retreat or, if you are unable to do so, visit as a day guest.

We hope you'll join us for a time of spiritual refreshment, meaningful friendships, and the simple joy of sharing life together in a beautiful natural setting.
