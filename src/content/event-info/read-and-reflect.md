**Wednesdays, 10am to noon, online via Zoom.** Hosted by the Swedenborg Centre (an online service from 1 December 2026).

You're warmly invited to join our weekly Swedenborg reading and exploration on Wednesdays. Together we read and discuss passages from Emanuel Swedenborg's writings, taking time to reflect on their spiritual meaning and how they can enrich our understanding of everyday life. Whether you are new to Swedenborg or have been reading his works for many years, you are very welcome.

We are currently exploring *True Christianity* (also published as *True Christian Religion*), Swedenborg's comprehensive presentation of his central theological teachings. Each session focuses on a small section of the text, with plenty of opportunity for questions, discussion, and sharing insights.

There's no need to have attended previous sessions. We begin each week with a brief overview of the context, making it easy to join at any point in the series.

Come along with curiosity, bring your questions, and enjoy exploring these profound ideas in a friendly and welcoming group.
