Howard Thompson reprints two of his most-read blog posts: \"Beyond the Veil,\" using a mountaineer's near-death fall to explore Swedenborg's account of what the spirit retains and loses after death, and \"If God Exists, Where Is My Freedom?\", a sharp answer to the classic objection that a divine presence must cancel out human freedom. Elsewhere, Howard reviews James Clear's Atomic Habits through the lens of an old Lao Tzu quote on how thoughts become destiny, tying self-improvement back to New Church ideas about character. Also inside: news of the Centre's revived website and blog, and a Riley Lee and Cliona Molins concert at the church that August."
