Howard's \"Providence\" essay traces how Divine Providence is only ever visible in hindsight, through two personal stories - a Crosby, Stills & Nash lyric that confirmed his call to Australia, and how his brother's death by suicide decades earlier quietly reshaped the whole course of his life. His companion post-Easter reflection, \"As He Died to Make Men Holy,\" reframes \"laying down one's life\" away from heroic battlefield imagery and toward the harder, daily work of surrendering pride and self-will. Debra's own warm, funny goodbye closes out the issue."
