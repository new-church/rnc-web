## 1. Our commitment

The Sydney Society of the New Church [ABN/ACN or incorporated association number
to be confirmed] (**we**, **us**, **our**) is the organisation behind Roseville
New Church. We value the trust our members, visitors, supporters and the wider
community place in us.

This policy explains how we collect, use, hold, disclose and protect personal
information, and how you can access it, correct it or make a complaint. We handle
personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles in the
*Privacy Act 1988* (Cth).

**Personal information** means information or an opinion about an identified
individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable.

## 2. What personal information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

- **Contact and identity details** — name and title, postal address, email
  address, home, mobile and work phone numbers, and your country or location.
- **Relationship and membership information** — whether you are a member of the
  Roseville New Church or the Swedenborg Association of Australia, membership
  and committee roles, the year you joined, subscription and payment status,
  Swedenborg Centre library membership, and the mailing preferences you have
  chosen (for example, how many copies of *Candela* or the church calendar you
  receive, and whether you receive AGM reports).
- **Family and pastoral information you choose to share** — for example, family
  relationships, birthdays and children's details recorded for our birthday and
  children's programs, and information shared with a pastor in the course of
  pastoral care.
- **Event and activity information** — registrations, attendance, dietary or
  accessibility requirements, and photographs taken at church events.
- **Donation and financial information** — records of donations, subscriptions
  and payments, and the details needed to process and receipt them.
- **Correspondence** — enquiries and messages you send us through the website,
  by email, by phone or in person, including any questions you put to a chatbot
  or similar tool on the site.
- **Website and technical information** — see section 6.

We generally collect only what we need for the purposes in section 4.

### Sensitive information

Because we are a religious organisation, some of the information we hold is
**sensitive information** under the Privacy Act — most obviously the fact of your
association with the New Church and your participation in its religious
activities. We may also come to hold health or accessibility information you
give us so we can support you at an event.

We collect sensitive information only where it is reasonably necessary for our
activities, and either you have consented, or the collection falls within an
exception in the Privacy Act (including the exception allowing a non-profit
organisation to collect sensitive information relating to its activities where it
relates to its members or to individuals who have regular contact with it).

### Children's information

We record limited information about children — for example, names and birthdays
for children's programs and birthday cards — and do so with the consent of a
parent or guardian.

## 3. How we collect personal information

We collect personal information:

- directly from you — in person, at services and events, by phone, by email,
  through forms on our website, or when you subscribe to a newsletter or make a
  donation;
- from our historical membership and contact records, including records
  maintained by the Society over many years and imported into our current
  systems;
- from people acting on your behalf, or from family members, where it is
  reasonable in the circumstances (for example, a family member passing on a
  change of address); and
- from related New Church bodies, such as [the Swedenborg Centre / Swedenborg
  Association of Australia / the New Church in Australia], where you have dealt
  with them and it is reasonable for information to be shared for a related
  purpose.

If we receive personal information about you that we did not ask for and could
not lawfully have collected, we will destroy or de-identify it where the law
requires.

You can deal with us anonymously or under a pseudonym where it is lawful and
practicable — for example, when making a general enquiry — although we may not be
able to provide everything you ask for.

## 4. Why we collect, hold and use personal information

We use personal information to:

- run church services, groups, classes and events, and to provide pastoral care;
- maintain the Society's membership roll and contact directory, and administer
  memberships and subscriptions;
- communicate with you — newsletters (including *Candela*), the church calendar,
  event notices, AGM papers, birthday greetings and general correspondence;
- process, receipt and acknowledge donations, and meet our record-keeping
  obligations;
- administer the Society's governance, including meetings, elections and reports
  to members;
- operate, maintain and improve our website;
- respond to your enquiries; and
- comply with our legal obligations, including under the *Associations
  Incorporation Act 2009* (NSW) [if applicable] and taxation and charity
  regulation.

We only use personal information for the purpose we collected it, for a related
purpose you would reasonably expect, or where you have consented or the law
allows or requires it.

## 5. Direct marketing and communications

We send newsletters, event invitations and appeals to members, supporters and
others who have asked to hear from us. We will only send commercial electronic
messages consistent with the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth).

Every email newsletter includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out of any
of our communications at any time by contacting us using the details in section
13. We will action opt-out requests promptly and at no cost. Note that if you
opt out of general mailings we may still need to send you communications
required by law or by our rules, such as notices of general meetings if you are
a member.

## 6. Website, cookies and analytics

When you visit our website we (or our service providers) may automatically
collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type and version,
device type, the pages you visit, the date and time of your visit and the site
you came from. This is used for security, troubleshooting and to understand how
the site is used.

**Cookies.** Our site uses cookies — small files stored on your device. We use:

- *essential cookies*, needed for the site to work, including keeping
  administrators signed in to the secure administration area; and
- [*analytics cookies*, which help us understand site usage in aggregate — to be
  confirmed and listed here, or this line removed if no analytics are used].

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, but some parts of
the site may not work properly if you do.

**Embedded and third-party content.** Some pages include content or links from
third parties (for example, mapping, calendar, video or newsletter services).
Those parties may collect information about you under their own privacy policies,
which we do not control.

**Chatbot.** If an AI-assisted chatbot is available on the site, your questions
are processed by [provider to be confirmed] to generate a response. Please do
not enter sensitive personal information into it.

## 7. Who we disclose personal information to

We may disclose personal information to:

- our office-bearers, pastors, staff and volunteers, on a need-to-know basis for
  the purposes above;
- our service providers, who handle information on our behalf under contract —
  including our website host and platform provider, our database and cloud
  storage provider, our email newsletter provider [Mailchimp], our mail-house or
  printing provider [PostGrid], accounting and payment providers, and IT support;
- related New Church bodies, where you would reasonably expect it (for example,
  in connection with joint events or shared publications) or where you have
  consented;
- professional advisers such as auditors, accountants and lawyers; and
- government agencies, regulators, courts or law enforcement, where required or
  authorised by law.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not disclose it to third parties
for their own marketing purposes.

### Publications and directories

Where we publish a member directory, service roster, event photograph or an
article naming or picturing individuals, we do so with consent and will remove or
amend the material on request wherever practicable.

## 8. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Australia. For
example, our website hosting, database, email and newsletter providers may hold
data in [the United States, Singapore, the European Union — to be confirmed].

Where we disclose personal information to an overseas recipient, we take
reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with the Australian Privacy
Principles, including through contractual protections with those providers.

## 9. How we hold and protect personal information

Personal information is held in electronic systems (our contact database, cloud
storage, email and newsletter platforms) and, for older records, in paper files
kept at the church and the Swedenborg Centre.

We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, interference, loss, and
unauthorised access, modification or disclosure — including access controls and
individual logins for our administration area, restricting access to those who
need it, secure (encrypted) connections to our website, provider-side encryption
and backups, and guidance for office-bearers and volunteers on handling
information.

No system is completely secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to result
in serious harm, we will respond in line with our breach response procedure and,
where the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme applies to us, notify affected
individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

## 10. Retention and destruction

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above,
or as required by law — for example, financial and governance records that must
be retained under taxation, charity or associations legislation.

We also maintain archival records of the Society's history, including membership
and congregational records dating back to its founding in [1875]. These are
retained for historical, genealogical and archival purposes.

When information is no longer needed and we are not required to keep it, we
destroy it securely or de-identify it.

## 11. Accessing and correcting your information

You may ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask
us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or
misleading. Contact us using the details in section 13.

We will respond within a reasonable period — usually within 30 days. We may need
to verify your identity first. Access is free, though we may charge a reasonable
fee for the cost of retrieving and copying extensive records; we will tell you
before any cost is incurred.

We may refuse access or correction in the limited circumstances permitted by the
Privacy Act — for example, where granting access would unreasonably affect
another person's privacy, or where the information relates to confidential
pastoral communications involving others. If we refuse, we will tell you why in
writing and explain how to complain.

## 12. Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information or breached the
Australian Privacy Principles, please contact our Privacy Officer using the
details below. We will acknowledge your complaint promptly, investigate it, and
respond in writing — usually within 30 days.
